Privacy

What this site collects, why, and what happens to it. Last updated September 25, 2026.

Messages

When you use the contact form, we get your name, your email, the print you ask about and your message. It goes to Kawika's inbox and to a private spreadsheet so nothing gets lost. We use it only to answer you.

Orders

Checkout is handled by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach us. We receive your name, email, phone number and shipping address so we can make and deliver your print, and we share them with our local print lab only when they need them to fill your order.

Analytics and cookies

This site doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records visitors' IP addresses for security, as most hosts do. Fonts and images are served from this site, not from third parties.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't send marketing email unless you ask for it.

Your information

Want to see what we have or have it deleted? Email info@kawikalopez.com and we'll take care of it.
