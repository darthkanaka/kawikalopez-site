See it on your wall before you buy

Every print page shows the print to scale in a real room. Switch sizes and materials and it updates, so you know how 48 inches of Diamond Head will sit over your sofa before it arrives.

Try it with Kaimana

Printed on Oʻahu, ready to hang

Canvas

Archival pigment inks on museum-quality canvas, gallery-wrapped so it hangs without a frame. Soft texture, no glare.

Metal

Dye-sublimated onto ChromaLuxe aluminum made in the USA. Deep color, crisp detail and a finish that lasts.

Pickup or shipping

Each print is made to order locally on Oʻahu, usually in about a week. Pick it up free on the island, or have it shipped anywhere in the US.

About the photographer

Kawika Lopez grew up on Oʻahu and has spent the last decade photographing it from the ridgelines and from the air. Most of these prints were made at sunrise, after a hike in the dark or a drone flight along the coast, and each one comes with the story of how it was made.
