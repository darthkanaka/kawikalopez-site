## Pick the part of the island they'll call home

Start with the place closest to their new home, or the view they love most. Not every buyer wants the beach outside their own door. Some want the most beautiful part of the island on their wall, wherever they live.

For buyers in town, Honolulu and Diamond Head are the views they'll know by heart.

- **Pālolo, Kaimukī and Mānoa.** [Mauka i Makai](print:043) looks from a ridge above Pālolo across Honolulu to Diamond Head and Waikīkī at golden hour.
- **Waikīkī and the condos in town.** [Nani Waikīkī](print:naniwaikiki) is the city and Diamond Head washed in pink at the end of the day.
- **Kāhala, Kapahulu and Diamond Head.** [Kaimana](print:kaimana) is Diamond Head and Waikīkī from the air at sunrise, with gold light on the crater.
- **Anywhere in Honolulu.** [Lēʻahi Kahiko](print:leahikahiko) is the crater at first light with the city beyond, and it comes as a $55 matted print too.

[prints: kaimana, leahikahiko]

Outside town, pick the beach or the ridge closest to their new place.

- **Kailua and Waimānalo.** [Waimānalo](print:waimanalo) is the whole beach from above, all the way to the cliffs.
- **Hawaiʻi Kai and the east side.** [Makapuʻu Beach](print:makapuubeach-h) is the teal water and white sand, straight down.
- **The windward side.** [Mokoliʻi Reflection](print:mokoliireflection) is the little island off Kualoa on a still morning.
- **The North Shore.** [Mālie](print:malie) is water spilling over a reef at Papailoa Beach, and it's one of Kawika's favorites.

## Get it to the closing on time

Pickup on Oʻahu is free and usually ready 7 to 10 business days after you order. Order about two weeks before the closing and you can hand it over yourself, at the table or at the front door.

If they're moving in later, ship it straight to the new address once they're in. It arrives 7 to 14 business days after you order in Hawaiʻi, and nothing in the box shows a price. For a Christmas closing, order by December 4 for delivery in Hawaiʻi or December 10 for pickup.
