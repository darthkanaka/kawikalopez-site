On the windward side, Kailua and Lanikai look out at the Mokulua, two islets whose name simply means two islands. A little south, Waimānalo runs for miles of sand below the Koʻolau cliffs, and inland the sharp peak of Olomana rises over the rainforest of Maunawili.

There are two takes on the Mokulua here: [Mokulua](print:mokulua-islands) in full color from the air, and [Mokulua Monochrome](print:mokulua), where the reef lines in the sand lead the eye straight out to the islets. [Waimānalo](print:waimanalo) looks down the beach after a night of waves left a repeating pattern along the shore, and [Olomana](print:olomana) catches the peak above a layer of mist.
