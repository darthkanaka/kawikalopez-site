The Kaiwi coast is the dry, rocky stretch of southeast Oʻahu between Hanauma Bay and Makapuʻu, with Kalanianaʻole Highway winding along the cliffs above the water. It's often called one of the most scenic shorelines in the state, and it's a short drive from town.

[Hanauma](print:hanauma) looks down into the bay at sunrise, stitched together from nearly 20 frames. [Kaiwi](print:kaiwi) follows the highway's curves from straight above. Down at the waterline, [Frosted Tidepool](print:frostedtidepool) turns the ripples in a tide pool to frosted glass with a slow shutter, and [ʻEleu](print:eleu) catches a wave breaking at Sandy Beach as the sun comes up. [Kākau](print:032) is the eroded face of Koko Head from the air, its lines like a traditional Hawaiian tattoo.

At the eastern end of the coast is [Makapuʻu](place:makapuu).
