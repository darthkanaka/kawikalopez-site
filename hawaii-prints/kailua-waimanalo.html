Windward Oʻahu · Oʻahu

Lanikai, Kailua and Waimānalo prints

Prints of Oʻahu's windward side, including the Mokulua islets off Lanikai in color and in black and white, Waimānalo Beach from the air, and Olomana.

On the windward side, Kailua and Lanikai look out at the Mokulua, two islets whose name simply means two islands. A little south, Waimānalo runs for miles of sand below the Koʻolau cliffs, and inland the sharp peak of Olomana rises over the rainforest of Maunawili.

There are two takes on the Mokulua here: Mokulua in full color from the air, and Mokulua Monochrome, where the reef lines in the sand lead the eye straight out to the islets. Waimānalo looks down the beach after a night of waves left a repeating pattern along the shore, and Olomana catches the peak above a layer of mist.
