Shipping

We ship within the United States only. Shipping is one flat rate for the whole order, set by the size and material of the prints: one rate for addresses in Hawaiʻi and one for the mainland. Your address decides which rate applies.

Please double check your address. If a print comes back because the address was wrong, we'll ship it again for the cost of shipping.

Prints larger than 60 x 30 inches are pickup on Oʻahu only. If you need one shipped, get in touch and we'll quote freight.

Free pickup on Oʻahu

Choose free pickup at checkout and we'll email you when your print is ready to set a time. Visiting? Order a week or two before you fly home and pick it up while you're here.

Cancelling an order

Changed your mind? Email us within 24 hours of ordering and we'll cancel and refund you in full. After that your print is usually in production, and we can't take it back.

All sales final, except damage or defects
