Canvas or metal

Canvas

Printed with archival pigment inks on museum-quality canvas, then gallery-wrapped so it hangs clean without a frame. The texture is soft and there's no glare, so it's easy to live with in a bright room.

Metal

Dye-sublimated onto ChromaLuxe aluminum made in the USA. Colors run deep, detail stays sharp and the surface holds up for years. It suits the ocean and sky shots especially well.

How big should you go?

Above a sofa, a bed or a console, aim for a print about two thirds the width of the furniture. Over an 84 inch sofa that's about 56 inches, so a 48 x 16 panorama or a 36 x 24 print sits well. On an open wall with nothing under it, go bigger than feels safe. Most people end up wishing they had.

Hang it so the middle of the print is about 57 to 60 inches from the floor, or leave 8 to 10 inches between the bottom of the print and the top of the furniture.

Still not sure? Every print page shows that print to scale on a wall, and you can switch sizes to compare.
