About this photograph

Literally translated to "Island Jump," this image entitled Mokulele was taken from the small islet off the Kaʻaʻawa coast during sunrise. Without being familiar with the geography, you wouldn't know that the rocky formation and tiny sand beach in the foreground was part of a tiny islet. The contrasting colors of the sand and rocks lead the eyes up towards the dramatic and iconic ridge line of Kaʻaʻawa Valley, a.k.a. "Jurassic Valley," for its repeated use in the Jurassic Park film saga.

Photographed at Mokoliʻi (Chinaman's Hat), Oʻahu.
