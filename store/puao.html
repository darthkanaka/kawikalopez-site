About this photograph

Using a drone and stitching several exposures together, I created this image of a specific section of the Koʻolau Mountain Range on Oʻahu. This specific facade really stood out to me due to its geometrical symmetry combined with the dramatic light from one side. I chose to name this piece Pūʻao, translates to womb. Although typically pointing down, the triangle is often associated as a feminine symbol of strength and fertility. This image of Mother Earth and her fertile body and lush forests show off the miracle that is the female body.

Photographed at The Koʻolau Mountains, Oʻahu.
