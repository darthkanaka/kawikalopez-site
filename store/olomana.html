About this photograph

Olomana's sharp peak, the landmark above Kailua and Maunawili, seen from the air. Mist hangs over the rainforest below, and the Koʻolau cliffs rise behind it. A tall print for a narrow wall.

Photographed at Kailua, Lanikai and Waimānalo, Oʻahu.
