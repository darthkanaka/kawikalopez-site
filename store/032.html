About this photograph

This image was created using a drone along the Kaiwi Coast on Oʻahu. A kākau, or "tattoo" common among native Hawaiians. Native Hawaiian body art often include repeating lines, triangles, and other patterns. As I flew the drone over this particular face of Koko Head Crater, I couldn't help but notice the similarities in the landscape to a traditional Hawaiian kākau.

Photographed at The Kaiwi Coast, Oʻahu.
