About this photograph

This image was created during sunrise using a drone. As I flew along the cliffs of Makapuʻu, I noticed the symmetrical nature of the geography as well as the contrast of light and dark, which combined for an interesting combination. Add in a lighthouse and you get an interesting combination of elements. While editing the image to emphasize the contrast in colors and tone while maintaining a symmetrical composition, I wanted to play off of the idea that there are often two very different sides of the same coin. Makapuʻu lighthouse is perched on the eastern tip of Oʻahu.

Photographed at Makapuʻu, Oʻahu.
