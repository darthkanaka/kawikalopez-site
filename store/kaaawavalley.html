About this photograph

After multiple trips out to the east side, I finally caught a fairly clear morning where the clouds were obscuring sections of the mountain ridges. This incredible view of Kaʻaʻawa valley is one of my personal favorites. The stunning shapes, sheer cliffs, and dramatic islets make for such a compelling and captivating image to be displayed in a common room such as the living room or large entry way.

Photographed at Kaʻaʻawa Valley and Kualoa, Oʻahu.
