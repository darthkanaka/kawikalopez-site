Metal

Dye-sublimated onto ChromaLuxe aluminum made in the USA. Colors run deep and detail stays sharp, and the surface holds up for years.

Canvas

Printed with archival pigment inks on museum-quality canvas, then gallery-wrapped so it hangs clean without a frame. A soft texture and no glare, good for bright rooms.

Matted print

A smaller, simpler way to bring one of these photographs home. It's printed here on Oʻahu on 275-gram satin photo paper, with rich color and a smooth, scratch-resistant finish. It's set in an acid-free white mat, 11 x 14 on the outside, with a backing board and a clear sleeve to keep it clean. Vertical photos are 8 x 10 and horizontal ones are 8 x 12, and both fit any standard 11 x 14 frame.

Prints ship up to 60 inches on the long side. Larger sizes are pickup on Oʻahu only; get in touch and we'll quote freight.
