About this photograph

This aerial landscape image was created by stitching 4 images together from a drone. On this morning, the overnight waves had created a beautifully organic pattern on the shoreline, with repeating indentations going all the way down the beach. Only from the air could this pattern be fully appreciated. Pair that with a dramatic cliffside in the distance and it makes for a stunning image. Waimānalo is known for its long sandy beaches, picturesque offshore islets and clear blue waters.

Photographed at Kailua, Lanikai and Waimānalo, Oʻahu.
