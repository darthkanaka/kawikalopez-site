About this photograph

Created a few minutes before sunrise, the light illuminated only a small portion of the lowest clouds in the sky, creating a dramatic contrast. The lone offshore islet centered alone in the open ocean to create a sense of quietness and tranquility. This image was made by stitching 3 images together in a vertical panoramic.
