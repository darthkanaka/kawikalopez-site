About this photograph

Mauka i makai means from the mountains to the sea. From a ridge above Pālolo Valley, the view runs across Honolulu to Diamond Head and the Waikīkī skyline in golden hour light, the whole south shore in one wide frame.

Photographed at Diamond Head and Waikīkī, Oʻahu.
