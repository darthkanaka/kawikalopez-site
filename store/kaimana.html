About this photograph

Literally translated to “Diamond,” this piece was taken during sunrise showing the splendor of Diamond Head in golden fashion. This panoramic was stitched together from nearly 12 images in order to achieve its wide field of view.

Photographed at Diamond Head and Waikīkī, Oʻahu.
