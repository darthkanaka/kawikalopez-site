Questions

Sending it away

Can I ship it straight to them?

Yes. Put their address in at checkout and it goes straight to them, with no price in the box.

How long does it take to reach the mainland?

Each print is made to order in about a week, then takes 10 to 21 business days to arrive. For Christmas, order by Nov 20.

What's easiest to send?

A matted print. It ships flat for $13 and fits any standard 11 x 14 frame they pick up near home.
