Questions

Before you order

Will they see what I paid?

No. Nothing in the box shows a price.

How long does it take?

Every print is made to order, usually in about a week. Delivery takes 7 to 14 business days in Hawaiʻi and 10 to 21 to the mainland. Pickup on Oʻahu takes 7 to 10.

What if it arrives damaged?

Send a photo within 7 days and it's replaced free. Otherwise all sales are final, and you can cancel within 24 hours.

Canvas, metal or matted?

Metal is bright and modern and Kawika's favorite. Canvas is softer and warmer. A matted print is the smallest and goes in any standard 11 x 14 frame.
