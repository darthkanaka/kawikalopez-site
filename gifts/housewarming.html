Questions

Before you buy for a new home

What size should I get?

The middle size fits most walls. For a hallway or above a desk, go a size down. For a big empty living room wall, go up.

Can I pick it up instead?

Yes, free on Oʻahu, usually 7 to 10 business days after you order.

Do they need anything to hang it?

Canvas comes with a hanger and metal with eyelets, so a nail or two does it. A matted print goes in any standard 11 x 14 frame.
