Questions

Buying for Dad

Will it fit his office?

The middle size fits most walls. Above a desk or in a narrow spot, go a size down.

Can it ship straight to him?

Yes, anywhere in the US. Put his address in at checkout, and nothing in the box shows a price.

Canvas or metal?

Metal for an office, a den or anywhere with a modern look. Canvas if his place is warmer and softer.
