Questions

Questions realtors ask

Who can sign up?

Hawaiʻi real estate agents and brokers, with the email address their brokerage gave them.

Does 15% come off everything?

Yes. Every print, in every size, on canvas, metal or matted, every time you order.

Can I use it with another code?

No. Your 15% can't be combined with other codes.

How do I get it to the closing?

Pick it up free on Oʻahu, usually 7 to 10 business days after you order, so order about two weeks ahead. Or ship it straight to the buyers' new address. Nothing in the box shows a price.

What do you do with my information?

It's used for your account and your realtor price, and nothing else. The privacy policy has the details.
